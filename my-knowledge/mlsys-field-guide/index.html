primary source

RoPE relative-position property

Paired rotation q′m = R(m)qm
k′n = R(n)kn

(R(m)q)ᵀ(R(n)k) = qᵀR(n−m)k

Because R(m)ᵀR(n)=R(n−m), the rotated dot product depends on relative displacement. That does not mean every RoPE frequency simply decays with distance.

Repository boundary

OLMo-core implements text RoPE plus ABF, PI, stepwise, and YaRN scaling. Its vision encoder uses learned absolute positions with interpolation, not Qwen-style 2D/M-RoPE.

Lab console · relative-position check

Rotate random q and k at positions m and n. Numerically verify the dot product equals the unrotated pair with angle n−m; then break the pair layout and observe the invariant fail.
