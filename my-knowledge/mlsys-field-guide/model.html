KV heads are a serving decision made in architecture.

MHA, GQA, and MQA keep the query-head count while changing how many key/value streams must be projected, stored, and read during autoregressive decoding.

GQA selected: 40 query heads share 8 KV heads in groups of 5. The cache is 20.0% of same-shape MHA before sharding and allocator overhead.

KV-cache calculator

shape-derived
bytes = 2 · B · L · S · Hkv · dh · bytes/element

The factor 2 is K plus V. Queries are computed for the current token and are not retained in the KV cache.

OLMo-core allocates K and V as [B,S,Hkv,dh], then repeats them logically when a backend needs one K/V view per query head. attention/kv_cache.py:11–40 · attention/backend.py:352–365, 1136–1145. OLMo2-32B uses 40 query / 8 KV heads; the Llama2-like 70B config uses 64 / 8.
Do not overclaim. Reducing KV heads shrinks K/V projection parameters and cache bandwidth. It does not reduce query heads, and end-to-end attention FLOPs do not fall by the same ratio.

A sparse router needs two different kinds of pressure.

Top-k decides which experts execute. Load balancing discourages collapse; router z-loss controls logit scale. DeepSeek-V3’s bias controller changes the first mechanism, but does not make every auxiliary balance term disappear.

DeepSeek caveat. DeepSeek-V3 uses a non-gradient per-expert bias for its main batch-wise load control: the bias affects top-k selection, while the original affinity supplies the gate weight. It still adds a tiny complementary sequence-wise balance loss, reported as α = 0.0001. “Auxiliary-loss-free” does not mean “no auxiliary balance term anywhere.”
Two z-losses live in this checkout. MoE router z-loss acts on expert logits. LM softmax z-loss acts on vocabulary logits. Both square a logsumexp, but they regularize different distributions.
Exact trails: moe/router.py:303–325, 339–359, 416–512 · moe/loss.py:29–138 · functional/cross_entropy_loss.py:12–50. OLMo’s optional shared MLP is a useful analogue, but its fixed mixing is not an exact DeepSeekMoE reproduction.
