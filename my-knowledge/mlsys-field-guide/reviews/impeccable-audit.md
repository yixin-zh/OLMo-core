# Impeccable detector audit

Date: 2026-08-13  
Invocation policy: the detector was run exactly once after all substantive UI and review fixes.

## Findings

The one-shot scan returned warnings for repeated one-rem spacing in the compact training surface, a compressed utility type hierarchy, aphoristic “No …” systems copy, and three thick left-edge report accents. It also returned advisories for literal tonal colors, compact font sizes, a 4 px compact radius, and 999 px semantic capsules that were not fully represented in the design-system primitive list.

## Disposition

- The three report side tabs were replaced with full diagnostic borders and thick top rules.
- Training workbench spacing now varies at 1.25 and 1.5 rem where sections change.
- Systems primary readouts/citations and Labs explanatory copy use a clearer body step; terse feature-boundary sentences were rewritten as complete prose.
- Guided-lab colors now reuse `amber-deep` rather than introducing literal semantic colors.
- `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` now explicitly document the 0.58–0.92 rem micro-type utility range, tonal-ramp literals, 4 px compact control radius, and 999 px capsule radius.

The detector was not rerun. This record therefore distinguishes confirmed corrections from a claimed zero-warning rescan. Final validation relies on source inspection, static checks, browser review, and a fresh finish reviewer.
